## Key Insights

- **Total Sales ₹15.58 Cr, Total Profit ₹3.32 Cr** (profit margin 21.3%), 10,000 orders aur 24,926 units se (Jan 2024 – Aug 2026).
- **Electronics & Mobiles sabse bada category hai:** 79.3% sales (₹12.36 Cr) aur 79% profit isi se aata hai. Baaki 4 categories milake sirf ~21% hain.
- **Top 5 products sab Electronics se hain:** Laptop Backpack, Power Bank 20000mAh, 5G Smartphone, Wireless Earbuds aur Smartwatch (har ek ~₹2.3–2.6 Cr).
- **UPI sabse popular payment method hai:** 50.4% sales, aur Cash on Delivery sirf 14.8% hai.
- **Amazon FBA se 69% sales aati hai,** Seller Flex se 20% aur Merchant (FBM) se 11%.
- **Top 3 states:** Maharashtra (19%), Karnataka (15.7%) aur Delhi (14.4%), yaani teeno milake ~49% sales.
- **Order status:** 81% orders Delivered hain, 5.1% Returned aur 4.9% Cancelled. Returned aur Cancelled orders se ~₹1.56 Cr sales ka profit zero hai.
- **Sales trend:** 2024 (₹5.70 Cr) aur 2025 (₹5.74 Cr) lagbhag barabar rahe. 2026 ka data sirf August tak hai (₹4.14 Cr).

## Recommendation
- Electronics par zyada dependency risk hai, isliye Home & Kitchen aur Apparel ko badhane par focus karna chahiye.
- Return aur cancellation (~10%) kam karne se profit seedha badhega.
